INTERPRETATION CAUTIONS
-----------------------
* These are exploratory observational associations; correlation does not show HR caused the behavioral change.
* The current sample is small, so effect estimates can be unstable.
* Spearman rho is primary because learning-rate k and change scores can be skewed.
* BH-FDR is applied separately to the learning-rate and strategy hypothesis families.
* Win-stay and lose-switch are conditional behaviors after different feedback types, not mutually exclusive choices.
