| Panel | What it shows | What to look for |
**Left · Full recording**
    Both time-series traces across the recording. The x-axis is elapsed time and the y-axis is centered EEG amplitude. 
        The shaded strip marks the excerpt shown in the middle.
    Look for big changes in amplitude, bursts, or gaps. This gives you context for whether the zoomed excerpt 
        comes from a quiet or unusually noisy part of the recording. 
**Middle · Zoomed excerpt**
    About two seconds of the same samples before and after filtering, on the same axes. 
    Look for a fast, regular ripple that gets smaller after filtering while slower waveform features look similar. 
        This shows what the filter changed in the waveform itself. 
**Right · Power spectral density (PSD)** 
    How much power the signal contains at each frequency, averaged over matched four-second windows. 
        The shaded band marks **59–61 Hz**. 
    Look for a peak near **60 Hz** in the gray curve that drops in the teal curve. Then check whether the curves 
        still overlap through your **theta, alpha, and beta** ranges.
