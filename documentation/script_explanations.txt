plot_group_average_curve.py
    1. Loads precomputed individual learning rates (k) from learning_rates.csv.
    2. Performs a median split on k to classify subjects as 'good' or 'bad' learners.
    3. For each group, calculates the mean proportion of set‑winner choices for each trial position (1‑18) across all blocks and subjects.
    4. Fits an exponential (diminishing‑returns) curve: P(t) = a - b·exp(-k·t) to the group‑average data.
    5. Plots the fitted smooth curve with a shaded band representing the interpolated standard error of the mean (SEM) of the observed proportions.
    6. Saves the figure as 'group_average_exponential_learning_curves.png' in the learning_rates folder.
    Dependencies: pandas, numpy, matplotlib, scipy, statsmodels.

winstay_loseswitch_analysis.py
    This script calculates conditional win‑stay (stay after gain) and lose‑switch (switch after loss) proportions per trial (2‑18) for each subject and session.
    All proportions are conditional on the preceding feedback, which is the standard approach in reinforcement learning.
    Outputs a CSV summary and six subfolders:
        1_subject_bars_per_session/ : bar graphs per subject (overall win‑stay and lose‑switch for each session).
        2_subject_trial_by_trial/ : bar graphs per subject and session, two bars per trial (win‑stay, lose‑switch).
        3_group_average_bars_per_session/ : group average bars per session.
        4_group_average_trial_by_trial/ : group average bar graphs per trial (2‑18).
        5_good_vs_bad_by_feedback/ : bar plots comparing good and bad learners on “stay after gain” and “switch after loss”.
        6_subject_vs_group_comparison/ : for each subject, a bar graph comparing subject’s proportions with group averages (both sessions).
    Classification uses learning_rates.csv (session 1) or falls back to overall win‑stay proportion.

learning_slope_analysis.py
1. Loads raw trial data for sessions 1 (Pre) and 2 (Post).
2. Creates a trial‑level CSV with columns: Subject, Session, Block, Trial, Correct, LearnerType.
3. For each subject and session, estimates a learning slope (linear regression of
   proportion correct on trial number, using data aggregated across blocks).
4. Performs paired t‑tests and Cohen's d on slopes (Pre vs Post) for:
   - All subjects
   - Good learners only
   - Bad learners only
5. Fits a linear mixed model (LMM) on the raw trial‑level data:
   Fixed effects: Trial, Session, Trial × Session
   Random intercept: Subject
   Interpretation: negative Trial×Session[Post] coefficient = slower learning after stress.
6. Generates graphs: paired slopes, group learning curves, individual learning curves.
Dependencies: pandas, numpy, scipy, statsmodels, matplotlib
